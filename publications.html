Particle Acceleration in Magnetized Shear-Driven Turbulence

Mingxuan Liu, Mateusz Ruszkowski, Ellen Zweibel, Xiaochen Sun, Damiano Caprioli, Naixin Liang, S. Peng Oh, Anatoly Spitkovsky

The Astrophysical Journal accepted, 2026

MHD-PIC simulations of particle acceleration in sustained, subsonic, non-relativistic magnetized turbulence driven purely by velocity shear, with full particle backreaction. Acceleration continues only while the turbulence is driven. The mechanism is the systematic distortion of gyro-orbits by turbulent electric fields, and the mean energy gain scales quadratically with shear velocity.

Rayleigh–Taylor unstable flames: The effect of two-mode coupling

Mingxuan Liu, Elizabeth P. Hicks

Physical Review Fluids 9, 113203 (2024)

Adding a reaction to a Rayleigh–Taylor unstable interface changes how short-wavelength modes couple into long ones. Two-mode coupling destabilizes a metastable traveling wave and drives an inverse cascade that produces a mode with wavenumber GCD(k1, k2). We identify five distinct flame-growth solution types.
