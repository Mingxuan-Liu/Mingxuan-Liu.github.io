Dissipative Heating of Hα Filaments in Magnetized Turbulence

Mingxuan Liu, Mateusz Ruszkowski, Drummond B. Fielding, Philipp Kempski, Luca Comisso

2026 Manuscript completed · arXiv link forthcoming

Three-dimensional MHD simulations with explicit resistivity and viscosity show that magnetized turbulence concentrates dissipative heating in cold filaments. About two thirds of the explicitly dissipated power is deposited in gas at 8,000–30,000 K, with viscosity supplying roughly three quarters of that heating at unit magnetic Prandtl number. Combined Ohmic and viscous heating becomes comparable to radiative cooling near 104 K, supporting localized dissipation as a potential energy source for cluster filaments.
