Vanilla 香草

We adopted her from the humane society in Ann Arbor. They did not know her real birthday, so she was given a nominal one: 18 June 2024.

Click her. 0 pets

A DNA test puts her at 82% Siberian husky, 10% German shepherd and 8% Alaskan malamute, which mostly explains the coat and the opinions. The 18% does not seem to have changed much.

Her contribution to this thesis has been company and moral support, and in nearly two years she has not destroyed a single thing in the house. For a husky this is close to unheard of, and I do not intend to jinx it by writing more about it here.
